# Dollhouse connector room

`Workspace.Maze15.Connectors.DollhouseRoom_1` contains three miniature room models in `Miniatures`, at scales 1/4, 1/16, and 1/64. Each copy has three drawer models under `Furnishings.Wardrobe.Drawer`.

The nine miniature drawers do not carry the `Drawer` tag. The nine full-size room drawers under `DollhouseRoom_1.Furnishings` keep the tag.

All three miniature models are named `Dollhouse`, so their full paths are identical. Use the model scale to distinguish them.
